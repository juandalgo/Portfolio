Crisol

A deep dive into narrative game and level design.

Studio

Vermila Studios

Duration

Sep 2022 - Present

Engine

Unreal Engine 5

Role

Game & Level Designer

Overview

Crisol: Theater of Idols is a first-person action horror game set in Hispania, a twisted version of Spain. Play as Gabriel, a soldier who uses his own blood as ammunition. Explore Tormentosa, uncover dark secrets, and... survive.

I am currently working at Vermila Studios, where I have been contributing as a Game Designer and Level Designer on Crisol: Theater of Idols.

I joined the project at a very early stage and have been involved throughout the level design process, as well as a significant part of the gameplay design.

My Contributions

  • Designed and documented gameplay systems and features.
  • Developed combat mechanics, weapon feel, and gameplay balance.
  • Owned the combat design of the final boss encounter.
  • Defined the level structure and progression from initial concept to implementation.
  • Designed gameplay spaces that supported narrative delivery and player experience.
  • Established mission flow, pacing, and player progression throughout the level.
  • Created and iterated on combat encounters.
  • Implemented level scripting and gameplay sequences.
