About

There has always been a part of me that needed to create. As a child, it was absurd adventures with my toys. Later, I learned to write and discovered that I could share the stories I imagined. Then came drawing, and with it the ability to give shape to those worlds and scenes that until then only existed in my mind.

But then video games came along, and within them I found something unique that no other medium could offer, something that captivated me forever.

I fell in love with discovering interaction in video games, and how, when properly fused with the work itself, it turns the player from a spectator into an active part of the experience.

I am fascinated by the inherent idea that a video game is never truly complete until it is being played, because an essential part of it is the player’s interaction, and how the experience of a game is shaped through its mechanics, and above all, through how they are used and perceived by the player.

Of all creative mediums, video games are my favorite precisely because of their ability to turn the audience into an active part of the work. I am passionate about discovering new worlds, analyzing how their mechanics are designed to fit and enhance the rest of the experience, and understanding how each system contributes to the final result. I have always enjoyed asking not only what works, but why.

That is why I became a Game Designer. Because I fell in love with this aspect of video games from the moment I first picked up my cousin’s dusty, sticky SNES controller and became part of the world of Yoshi’s Island.

Let's build worlds together.
